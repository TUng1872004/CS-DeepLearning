Image Classification: FGVC-Aircraft Benchmark

🏆 Project Summaries & Achievements

Author: Vũ Minh Quân

Full Report: Report on Image Classification.pdf

Abstract

Fine-grained visual classification (FGVC) focuses on distinguishing between highly similar sub-categories within a broad category. This project benchmarks ResNet50 (CNN) and ViT-B/16 (Vision Transformer) on the FGVC-Aircraft dataset, evaluating three fine-tuning strategies: Linear Probing, Full Fine-Tuning, and Layer-wise Learning Rate Decay (LLRD). We investigate how architectural inductive biases and hierarchical optimization impact performance on 100 aircraft variants.

Key Achievements

  • Model Crossover: Identified the "crossover point" at 30 epochs where ViT overtakes ResNet in representation quality.
  • Optimization Excellence: Achieved a peak test accuracy of 88.9% using ViT-B/16 combined with LLRD and extended training (50 epochs).
  • Interpretability: Successfully mapped discriminative features (engines, wingtips) using Grad-CAM and Attention Rollout, validating model focus.

Methodology and Technical Highlights

  • Layer-wise LR Decay: Assigned hierarchical learning rates (decay=0.65) to Transformer blocks to protect low-level features.
  • Augmentation Robustness: Evaluated RandAugment impacts, finding that strong augmentation requires >40 epochs to surpass light regimes.

Limitations

  • ResNet50 plateaus earlier than ViT, suggesting a lower capacity for complex fine-grained features.
  • ViT models exhibit 3x higher inference latency, making them less ideal for real-time mobile deployment.
